# Fathomflare

A deep-sea boss rush in one HTML file, built by a single prompt on Codex (GPT-6.2 Sol).

Play it: https://games.gradedprompts.com/fathomflare/

`index.html` is the unedited output of one run of the prompt. Nothing was changed by hand after the run.
SHA-256: `2e4811a902f94430baf5cdcadb486e67210d151f3bdf20550bf5adb0983a9566`

How it plays: a small yellow sub faces five giants of the deep, one after another: the Pale Bell, the Lantern Angler, the Coil Nautilus, the Gulper Eel and the Abyss Crown. Each has three phases of fixed bullet patterns, rings, spirals, walls and beams, so you can learn them. The sub fires by itself; you dodge. Only the white dot in the sub can be hit. Flying close to bullets fills the glow bar, and every 40 near misses earn a flare that clears the water around the sub. A dive starts with three lives and gains one for every boss beaten; a lost dive can retry the boss it ended at, and Practice opens any boss you have reached. Your best dive is saved in your browser.

Arrows or WASD move, Shift held moves slowly and shows the hit dot, X or Space fires a flare, P or Esc pauses, M toggles sound. With a mouse or a finger, press anywhere and drag: the sub moves as far as your pointer does. On a phone held upright it plays as a vertical shooter, sideways as on a computer, with a Flare button bottom right. It loads Three.js from a CDN, so it needs a connection.

The prompt that built it is being prepared for Graded Prompts: https://www.gradedprompts.com/prompts/apps
